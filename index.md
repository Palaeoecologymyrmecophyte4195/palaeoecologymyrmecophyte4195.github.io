## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

**The app won't open.** Make sure you have at least 5 GB of free disk space and close other demanding programs.

**Generation takes too long.** Longer descriptions and more complex requests take more time. Try shorter phrases first.

**No sound.** Check your computer's volume and make sure speakers are connected. In remiqora, check the audio output settings—choose your correct speaker from the dropdown menu.

**The AI seems stuck.** Cancel the current task and try again with a simpler description.
